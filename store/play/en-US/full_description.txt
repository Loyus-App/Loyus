The bakery card, the cinema card, the gym card, the hardware store card... They end up in a drawer, or at home on the one day you need them. Loyus puts them all in your phone, for good.

SCAN IT ONCE, DONE
• Point the camera at the card, or read the code from a photo or a screenshot.
• No barcode on the card? Type the number and Loyus draws the code.
• Over 400 known stores: your card gets the store's logo and color.

AT THE TILL, NO FUSS
• One tap shows the code full size, brightness up, screen kept awake.
• Codes sized for store scanners (GS1 specs), sharp to the pixel.
• Code too long? Rotate it across the whole screen, or copy the number.
• Works offline, even in the supermarket basement.

YOUR CARDS, YOUR ORDER
• Pin your favorites to the top.
• Sort by most used, recently used, A to Z or your own way.
• Find any card by store, owner or number.
• Add a note (PIN, member number), the owner's name and front and back photos.

ALWAYS AT HAND
• Shortcuts on the app icon to your three most used cards.
• Light or dark theme, six accent colors, grid or list.

YOUR DATA STAYS WITH YOU
No account, no ads, no trackers. Loyus doesn't even have internet access: everything stays on your phone. To switch phones or keep a copy, export your cards to a file.

Loyus is free and open source.
